Our Origins

Born from the Alpine Valleys of Provence

NÉA was conceived between Paris and Grasse with a singular conviction: luxury beauty should never demand compromise between pure biological safety and breathtaking efficacy.

After decades formulating for heritage Parisian houses, our founding biochemists partnered with French botanists to isolate potent cellular phytonutrients from wild alpine roses, peony roots, and organic camellia. The result is a sensorial dialogue of clean, skin-identical bio-lipids that celebrate your personal contour rather than mask it.
